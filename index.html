Bibliography
The Tübingen Irish Database of Irish Science Fiction (TDISF) strives to create a database that comprises a selection of Irish SF texts for all researchers to use. The database was created based on the observation that there is significantly more Irish science fiction than one might initially assume. However, these texts are hardly visible in either the broader context of Irish literature or in the context of the science fiction metatext. Yet, as highlighted in the works of Jack Fennell (see the bibliography), Irish science fiction is inextricably linked to the Irish cultural imaginary, as this genre uniquely negotiates tradition and modernity. Against this backdrop, the database aims to contribute to the visibility of Irish science fiction and thus underscore its significance.

TDISF is part of the program "Mehr innovative Lehre wagen!" and funded by the Division III Academic Affairs of the University of Tübingen. The database is being created in the context of two seminars taught by PD Dr. Raphael Zähringer: "Irish Science Fiction" (summer term 2025) and "Anthologising Anglophone Science Fiction" (winter term 2025/2026). On the one hand, the database draws on Jack Fennell's existing bibliography A Short Guide to Irish Science Fiction (2019), whose entries are to be converted into a database format. On the other hand, in line with approaches to research-based learning, students in both courses are searching for Irish SF texts themselves and creating additional entries for texts in a process that is validated by student peer review. Taking these two sources together, it is expected that by the end of the funding phase in spring 2026, approximately 200 database entries will have been compiled.
